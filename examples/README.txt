ScanBox Synthetic Example Images
================================

These images were generated specifically for ScanBox testing. They do not
depict real transactions, books, authors, businesses, or people.

  Photograph-description and stylized-text test.

fictional-cafe-receipt.png
  Document OCR and receipt-layout test.

The examples may be distributed with ScanBox and used in its documentation,
tests, demonstrations, and screenshots.
